Multi-Track Sachet Machine

High-speed packaging for small sachets.

Machine Overview

The Multi-Track Sachet Machine is a high-output solution designed for producing multiple small sachets simultaneously. By using a wider roll of packaging film and multiple forming tubes and sealing jaws, this machine can significantly increase production capacity compared to single-track machines. It is perfect for products like shampoo, ketchup, oil, and small-quantity powders.

Key Features

  • High production output with multiple lanes (2 to 10 tracks).
  • Precise sealing and cutting for a clean, professional finish.
  • Can handle a variety of liquid, paste, and powder products.
  • Independent lane control for maximum efficiency.
