Multi-Track Sachet Filling and Sealing Machine

High-speed packaging for small sachets of liquids, pastes, and powders.

Machine Overview

Multi Track sachet packing machine is most commonly used for filling and sealing liquid and paste like shampoo, sauce, whiskey, tomato ketchup, pickle, chutney, oil, glue, gel, scrub, cream etc. This Multi track sachet filling and sealing machine works with piston or solenoid valve as filler to highly precise quantity to be filled. It is highly efficient with high speed, low power consumption and low rejection rate. Multi track Pouch packing machine can be used to fill and seal small pouches for powders, commonly used in medicine or ayurvedic powder.

Our range of high speed Liquid Multi Track Pouch Packing Machine can produce up to 28,800 pouches per hour, with sizes ranging from 0.5 ml to 100 ml. Especially designed for packing liquid, semi-liquid and pastes, these high speed machines give a cutting edge technology in the packaging industry leading to an approximate of 10% saving on laminate.
