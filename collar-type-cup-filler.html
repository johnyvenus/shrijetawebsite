Machine Overview

The Collar Type Machine with Volumetric Cup Filler is an ideal solution for packaging free-flowing powders and granules with speed and accuracy. This machine is widely used for products like sugar, salt, tea, whole spices, seeds, and detergents. The volumetric cups ensure a consistent fill weight for each pouch, making it a reliable and cost-effective choice for many industries.

Key Features

  • High-speed, accurate, and reliable operation.
  • Volumetric cup filling system for consistent dosing.
  • User-friendly PLC control panel.
  • Robust construction for long-term durability.
